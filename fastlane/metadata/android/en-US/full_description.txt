The family food app. A recipe box, a weekly dinner plan, the grocery list that writes itself from that plan, and a quiet one-tap food diary for anyone in the house who wants their own numbers.

• Weekly plan — decide once, shop once
• A grocery list built from the plan, not typed twice
• Recipe box, with import from a pasted URL
• Food diary: your regulars are one tap on ANY day, not just today
• Barcode scanning, or type the digits — two doors, neither remembered
• A bundled USDA food spine: 13,652 foods, offline forever
• Optional downloadable barcode databases for offline lookups
• Encrypted backup with a 12-word recovery phrase

Network use is narrow and named: fetching one recipe page you paste, and Open Food Facts barcode lookups that send only the barcode. Day-to-day logging, planning and lists never touch the network.

No ads, no accounts, no analytics.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
